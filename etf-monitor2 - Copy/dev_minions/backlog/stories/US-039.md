## Task

Write `dev_minions/verification/US-039-qa.md`, the Codex procedure, with:

1. **Setup.** `pnpm build` (needed by both `qa-serve.sh` and the harness), `bash scripts/claude/qa-serve.sh start`, the harness command from US-036, and `qa-serve.sh stop` at the end, even after a failure.
2. **Capture matrix** (review §2 US-039 (1)), one numbered row per capture:
   - routes: `/`, `/etf/BTBETRETF`, `/admin`, `/admin/etfs`, `/admin/etfs/BTBETRETF/fields`, `/admin/ai`, `/admin/cron`, `/admin/operations`, `/chat`, `/health` (every page under `app/` today; the plan re-checks the list against `app/**/page.tsx`);
   - × locale RO and EN (the `NEXT_LOCALE` cookie, as `roles/qa.md` says);
   - × width 375 px and 1280 px;
   - × theme light and dark.
   How each theme is set: `etf-theme` in `localStorage` before load, and once per theme through the header toggle followed by a reload (which also shows the choice survives a reload, FR15 "switchable").
   Screenshots are saved outside the project (e.g. `/tmp/etf-qa-screens/US-039/<id>.png`), per `roles/qa.md`.
3. **Design comparison for `/`** (review §2 US-039 (3), DEC-020 §10; sprint D-10): four extra captures at the reference sizes, in EN (the PNGs' language), each paired with its PNG:
   - about 1200×560 light → `mockup-home-light.png`; about 1200×560 dark → `mockup-home-dark.png`; dark with the Customize panel open → `mockup-home-dark-customize.png`; 390 px wide → `mockup-home-phone.png`;
   - the filled table and the open panel come from the harness output; the live `/` (empty/error state in the same card, spec rule 8) and the live header come from `qa-serve.sh`;
   - the checklist lists what must match (DEC-020 §10: layout, order, spacing rhythm, hierarchy, alignment, second line of each cell, hover and active-pill states), what need not (pixels, sample data, the illustration note), the shipped-rule differences that are not deviations, and the two corrections to check explicitly (the phone header does not overlap; no date or number wraps).
4. **Contrast check per screen** (review §2 US-039 (2)): a method that runs inside the page in the headless browser (a snippet the checklist quotes in full, pasted into the browser's evaluate step, not a file in the repo). For every visible text element it computes the WCAG 2.x ratio of the computed text colour against the effective background (walking up to the first opaque background), and reports each element below 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text: at least 24 px, or at least 18.66 px bold), with the route, theme, selector, colours and ratio. It also reports the `:focus-visible` outline of the first focusable element against its surface below 3:1 (DEC-020 §3, §8).
5. **Verdict format** for `US-039-qa-run.md`: one table row per capture: id, route, locale, width, theme, screenshot path, contrast result (PASS or the failing elements), and for the four design captures `MATCH` / `DEVIATION: <what>`. Then the "For the user" list with the one JUDGMENT item.
6. **Fallback** when the QA environment has no headless browser (review §2 "using `qa-serve.sh`"; `roles/qa.md` categories): say so; mark the captures and the in-page contrast check as one `AUTO-PARTIAL` item that states exactly what remains; still run the automatic parts (`qa-serve.sh get` in both locales for every route, status 200 and the translated text; the offline contrast test of US-035, quoted with its command and exit code); and leave the look to the user's JUDGMENT item. Never install a browser or any tool into the project's `package.json` or lockfile.
