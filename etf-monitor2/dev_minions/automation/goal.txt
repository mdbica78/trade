Using the deliver-story skill, deliver the eligible stories of the current sprint in dev_minions/backlog/ one at a time. The goal is met when every story of the current sprint is Awaiting QA, Done or Blocked in dev_minions/status.md, or no story is eligible because the remaining ones wait on the user (decision, QA or escalation), AND dev_minions/HANDOVER.md describes the state and the next step. Prove it at the end by printing the sprint rows of status.md and the "Waiting on the user" section of HANDOVER.md. Never run git. Stop early, after running the handover skill, if a product or design decision is needed, or after 60 turns.
