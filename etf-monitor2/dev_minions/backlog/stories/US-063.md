# US-063 — Job runs table scrolls

Sprint 14. Depends on: none. The user's five Sprint 14 needs are the business requirements; this is BR5.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC1 (FR13; Sprint 14 BR5):** The run-history table is contained in a region with a bounded height of about 24rem, vertical scrolling, and a sticky header; job-run count no longer expands the page indefinitely.
- **AC2 (FR13; Sprint 14 BR5):** On narrow viewports, horizontal overflow is contained and scrollable within the run-history region.
- **AC3 (FR8.1, FR13; Sprint 14 BR5):** The region is keyboard-focusable and has an accessible label in both locales; light and dark themes preserve visible focus and contrast.
- **AC4 (FR8.1, FR13):** Tests prove bounded/vertical and horizontal overflow, sticky header, keyboard focus and localized label. The intentional markup change is named in the implementation plan; typecheck, lint, full suite and offline build pass.

## Data / schema impact
No schema change, migration, query change or data-model change.

## Scope
The PO's existing implementation is retained as the candidate, subject to the independent review and full gates in `verification/US-063-plan.md`.
